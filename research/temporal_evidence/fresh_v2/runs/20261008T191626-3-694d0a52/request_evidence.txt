LEGACY KEYS. The group headed "transition" is P5, the lead arm parallel in the DOWNSWING. The word transition is legacy terminology and does NOT mean the top or the direction-change moment: that moment is P4. Checkpoint names beginning with transition_ are scored on the P5 cell; they do not move P5 to the top. The group headed "downswing" is P6, the shaft parallel in the downswing. It does NOT mean the first cell of the downswing and it does NOT mean P5.
