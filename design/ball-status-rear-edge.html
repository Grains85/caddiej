Projected floor

Ball status, just behind the hitting strip

The mat stays a clean rectangle. The words sit on the turf beyond its far edge — the side away from the player — and a thin line is all that joins them to that edge. Flat colour, no fill, no gradient. The green lamp is the only saturated mark, and it lights when a ball is detected.

01

Feet

Each bracket stands on the rear edge, runs up beside the words, and turns in. The foot lies along the edge.

02

Calipers

A diagonal leaves the edge, rises to the top of the words, and turns in as a short tick.

03

Chamfer

The rear edge itself steps up on a bevel, passes over the words, and steps back down.

04

Collar

A shallow rounded lip. The line runs along the edge, sweeps over the words, and returns. Open toward the mat.

05

Horizon

A line at the height of the words, broken to let them sit in the gap. The break drops to the rear edge.

06

Index

A rounded line wraps the top and sides only. One short stem pins the open bottom to the middle of the edge.

07

Fiducial

An open tray under the words, a centre stem, and two alignment ticks on the edge. The quietest of the set.
